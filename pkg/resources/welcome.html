This installs three community projects that run Nintendo Switch games natively on Apple silicon:

  • Sky Recomp: The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword HD (macOS 15+)
  • Mush8: Mario Kart 8 Deluxe (macOS 14+)
  • Build Tears of the Kingdom: a helper that builds the game from your own files (macOS 26+)

None of them contain game data. Each one asks for your own dump, made from a Switch and a game you own. Untick anything you do not want on the next step.
